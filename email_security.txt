In this discussion we are going to learn how to navigate cybersecurity. In order to properly navigate cybersecurity you need to understand the fundamentals. Knowing these basic functions will determine your success in identifying when you are experiencing a cyber attack and how to prevent any further harm. The first tactic that is frequently used in cyber attacks is called Phishing. Phishing, according to
Stringhini, is described as "A subset of spam email where victims are persuaded that the email is from a legitimate source and then provide login credentials for their online banking, social media, or email accounts". This means that any email sent from an unverified source can try to incite an emotional response with a sense of urgency to persuade you to make an uninformed decision. Doing this can lead to harmful malware that can slower the functionality of your computer. Next, we will discuss social engineering. Social engineering is where a user is deceived into providing sensitive information or downloading malware. According to Kapersky "One tactic is to get you into a heightened emotional state such as being fearful or excited. This is because you are more likely to make rash decisions in a heightened (positive or negative!) emotional state". Social engineering is important because it keeps you more aware of these malicious tactics so you can identify them sooner and protect sensitive information. Now that you understand the basic fundamentals for avoiding future cyber attacks, here are some useful steps to take that can help you prevent any future cyber attacks. First, make sure that whatever email or website you click on is from a verified source. Doing this will prevent malware from infecting your computer. Malware that reaches your computer can cause the browser of computer to slow down in functionality so if you are ever skeptical of an infection, check if your computer is running normally. As stated by Kapersky, "One of the most common signs is reduced computing performance such as slow running processes, random random programs running in the background, and applications taking a long time to load". Recognizing these signs is the first step to catching a cyber attack. Secondly, you are going to want to scan your computer with an antivirus tool. An antivirus is a tool used to detect, prevent and remove any malicious malware. "Common antivirus programs include proprietary tools by Norton, Bit Defender, and McAfee" says Kapersky. "When choosing an antivirus program it is also important to consider your needs as programs often come with additional features such as password management, safe browsing extensions or parental controls" (Kapersky). Utilizing this tool will ensure a greater success in preventing malicious hardware on your computer. Antivirus features are pricey but there are also free options available such as Avast One Essential. Though this feature may not always detect an attack, it is important to frequently update your software often to keep it updated. In conclusion, understanding basic cyber attacks such as Phishing and using the social engineering system is a process that not only helps you identify when you are being attacked and how to proceed for further encounters. Utilizing the usage of an antivirus malware such as McAfee or Norton will help you prevent, detect and delete any harmful malware that may infect your computer and breach your data.
